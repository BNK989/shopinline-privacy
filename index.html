Privacy Policy

ShopInline: Live Product Editor for WooCommerce

Last updated: October 2026

The short version

  • ShopInline has no server of its own. We do not run analytics or tracking, show ads, or receive any of your data.
  • Your keys and settings are stored only in your browser.
  • Your product data goes to your own store. It goes anywhere else only when you use an optional feature that needs it, as listed below.
  • When you ask a question about your store's figures, those figures go to Google Gemini with your own key, as totals only: never a visitor, an order or a customer.
  • We do not sell or share data, and we do not use it for anything other than editing your products.

What is stored in your browser

Stored with chrome.storage on your device. Nothing here is synced to other devices or sent to us.

  • Your stores: each store's address, its WooCommerce REST API keys (Consumer Key and Secret) and, if you add one, a WordPress Application Password used to upload images.
  • Settings: interface language, editor layout, AI preferences and, if you add one, your Google Gemini API key.
  • Google connection (optional): which Google Analytics 4 property and Search Console site belong to which store, and the email of the Google account you connected. The Google sign-in token is kept only for the current browser session and is discarded when the browser closes.
  • Cached figures: sales and analytics numbers shown on product cards, kept for up to 12 hours so pages load faster.

Removing the extension deletes all of it. You can also remove a store or disconnect Google from the extension's settings at any time.

What is saved in your store

Your edits are saved to your own WooCommerce store, which is the point of the extension. With each edit, ShopInline also adds an entry to that product's change log, stored in your store as product data. The entry records what changed, when, and the email address of the Chrome profile that made the change, read through the Chrome Identity API. This lets a shop with several editors see who changed what. The log lives only in your store, which you control.

About the image hosts

When ShopInline cannot put an edited image directly into your store's media library, for example because your site blocks direct uploads, it can place the image on one of the free public hosts above so WooCommerce can import it from a link. The same hosts are used to fetch a readable copy of an image link you add when the server behind that link refuses to share it. An image placed there can be opened by anyone who has the link, and is kept according to that host's own terms.

ShopInline asks you before it sends any image to these hosts, and sends nothing if you decline. If you tick "Don't ask again", you can turn the question back on under Settings → Image uploads in the extension popup. To avoid this route entirely, stay logged in to WordPress in the same browser, or add a WordPress Application Password in the store's settings, so images upload straight to your store.

On your device only

Background removal and Chrome's built-in AI (used for alt text when available) run on your own computer. The images they process are not uploaded anywhere for this.

Third-party terms

Data you choose to send to Google is handled under the Google Privacy Policy and the Gemini API Additional Terms. Note that when you use a free-tier Gemini key, Google may use the content, including the store figures sent with a question, to improve its products.

Google user data

If you connect Google, ShopInline asks for read-only access to Google Analytics (analytics.readonly) and Search Console (webmasters.readonly), plus your basic profile (openid, email). It uses this only to show visits, conversion and search queries for your own products inside the extension, and to explain them when you ask. This data stays in your browser and is never transferred to us; no copy of it ever passes through us.

The one transfer is one you ask for. When you send a question in the Ask tab, or press "Ask about this" on a figure, the Analytics and Search Console figures that question is about are sent, together with your question, to the Google Gemini API under your own API key, so Gemini can answer it. Nothing is sent until you ask. What is sent is aggregated totals for your own store (visits, engagement, devices, channels, referring sites, campaign names, search terms), never data about an individual visitor. Google user data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not used for anything other than showing and explaining your own store's figures to you.

ShopInline's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Disconnecting Google in settings revokes the token.

Permissions, and why

  • storage: saves the settings described above on your device.
  • activeTab: lets the popup see which site you are on, and lets the keyboard shortcut open the editor on that tab.
  • scripting: loads the editor into tabs of a store you have just connected, and runs the optional "Create keys for me" step on your store's WooCommerce settings page.
  • contextMenus: adds the right-click "Assign to WooCommerce Field" menu on your stores.
  • offscreen: runs on-device background removal.
  • identity: signs in to Google when you connect Analytics, and reads your Chrome profile email for the change log.
  • Host access: fixed access to Gemini and the image hosts listed above. Access to your store's own website is asked for separately, one store at a time, when you add it.
